REAL GEOGRAPHY. A PLAYFUL PERSPECTIVE.

Welcome to Mauritius Life.

A geographic foundation with user-reference-inspired Little World styling. Authenticity is tracked separately for footprints, terrain, and visual materials.

What comes from OpenStreetMap?
The coastline, roads and building footprints are mapped data. Town shortcuts open curated views; elsewhere, nearby street tiles load as you explore. Coverage varies, and free-roam terrain is flat. Based on a Geofabrik / OpenStreetMap extract dated 12 November 2026. It does not show all buildings on the island.
What is stylized?
Building colours, roof details, windows, roadside trees and animated cars are illustrative. Building heights use available OSM heights or levels, otherwise estimates. The initial height scale is 1×. No artificial mountain peaks are added. A triangulated terrain surface follows OSM coastlines and receives real Mapzen Terrarium elevation when its online tiles load; otherwise the scene explicitly falls back to flat land. Vertical datums and source resolution vary, so this is not survey-grade.
How is it built?
Three.js scene, georeferenced OSM geometry, elevation sampling, procedural geometry and texture palettes. OrbitControls, picking and 1:10 internal metre-based scene scale. A software canvas renderer projects scene triangles when WebGL is unavailable. No fake road grid and no satellite imagery are supplied.
